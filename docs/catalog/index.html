The 23 systems you can pick in ScaffVisor's System list, grouped by grid: systems in the same group share widths, bay lengths and lift, so their parts fit the same layout; weights and article numbers differ by maker. How to read a sheet

  • Weightsguideline values from the manufacturer's list; * = estimated
  • Rulesties, braces and wall gap: from the source when it gives them, otherwise placeholders (said on the sheet)
  • Advantages, limitscommon practice and the manufacturers' own descriptions; not a structural assessment
  • Modelsthe .blend of every sheet is saved next to the add-on's catalog folder

When to use

  • Façades within the manufacturer's standard configuration (typically up to 24 m).
When not to use

  • Heavy loads beyond the deck's load class; walls that cannot take ties.

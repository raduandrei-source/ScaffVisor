Every scaffold system found in the collected documentation and in published manufacturer data, grouped by dimensions. Systems that share a grid (same widths, bay lengths and lift) are listed together: their parts fit the same layout, but weights and article numbers differ by maker. A system appears once even if several documents describe it.

The [systems catalog](catalog/) has one sheet per system you can pick, with a model, dimensioned views, when to use it and its sources. 
**In ScaffVisor** names the preset you pick in the *System* list. The others are documented here for later presets; a preset is added only when the parts list with weights is published.

## How to read the table

- *Parts with weight*: parts listed with a published weight / all parts listed for that system in the source.
- Imperial systems keep their foot-based bays (5, 6, 7, 8, 10 ft).
- Accessories shared by a maker across its systems (DSS planks, jacks, toeboards) are counted with the presets that use them.

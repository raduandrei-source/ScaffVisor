## What ScaffVisor is not

- **It is not a stability calculation.** It does not check loads, wind, ground bearing capacity or the strength of the anchors. Ties and braces follow indicative rules from the manufacturer's documents.
- **It does not replace the manufacturer's assembly manual** or the person responsible for the site.
- Weights are catalog values. Clones, other generations and local variants can differ.

## What it does not do yet

- One system is built in (Plettac SL 70, steel).
- Corners at angles other than about 90° are approximate, and a note says so.
- A chain of connected faces with different heights uses its tallest face for the whole chain.
- On sloping ground the scaffold follows the terrain with jacks, compensation frames and extra lifts. It does not step the bays across a wall, and it cannot go beyond the travel of the jacks.
- No brackets, separate stair towers, passages or roofs yet.
- Transport planning and stock inventory are planned.
- Tested headless on Blender 5.0; please report anything you see on newer versions.

## Report a problem

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/raduandrei-source/ScaffVisor/issues) and include the Blender version, the add-on version, and the notes shown under Result.
